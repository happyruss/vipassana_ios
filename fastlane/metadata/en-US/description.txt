Learn body sensation awareness—step by step—until you can practice on your own.

Vipassana Insight is a guided meditation app from Meditation Treks. Work through shamatha, anapana, and vipassana sessions in order, customize how long each session runs, then use the silent timer when you no longer need voice guidance.

ONE-TIME PURCHASE
No subscription. No account. No streaming. Your progress stays on your device.

THE PATH
• Introduction and sequential guided sessions
• Shamatha and anapana breath practices
• Body sensation awareness (vipassana)
• Metta (loving-kindness) to close
• Built-in silent timer with intro and closing bells

OFFLINE
All guidance is bundled in the app. Practice without Wi‑Fi or cellular data.

Vipassana Insight is the focused companion for learning this technique. When you want treks, nature, breathwork, and more, explore Meditation Treks on the web at meditationtreks.com.
